How Fly works

🗓️

Real flights

We check which airline flights flew your route on the same weekday last week, and assume they fly again, usually on the same aircraft.

🌬️

Live forecast

Each flight's path and cruise altitude is checked against NOAA's latest turbulence forecast for the time it'll be there.

✈️

Your aircraft

Bigger, heavier planes feel the same air as less bumpy, so every score is adjusted for the aircraft type.

💺

Best seat

Seats over the wing, near the plane's centre of gravity, move the least. We show where they are.

For comfort planning only, not a safety tool. Forecasts cover roughly the next 36 hours, and pilots routinely change altitude or route to avoid turbulence. Aircraft types and times come from last week's operations and can change. Climb and descent are not scored.
